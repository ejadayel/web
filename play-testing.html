Test Daily Ring on Google Play

Thank you for testing. Your email is already on the testers list, so all that is left is four steps on your phone, then fourteen days of using the app.

iPhone and iPad Android
  • About 5 minutesFrom the link to your first check-off
  • Your Android phoneSigned in with the Google account you gave us
  • 14 daysStay in the test that long; it is what Google asks
  1. 1

    Use the Google account you gave us

    Only the email address on the testers list can join. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture in the top corner, and check that it is the same Gmail or Google account. If not, tap the arrow beside it and switch.

    Several accounts on one phone is the most common reason the test does not show up.

  2. 2

    Open the test link and become a tester

    On your phone, open the link below. Make sure it opens signed in with that same account, then tap Become a tester. The page then says you are a tester.

    Open the test link

    It also works from a computer’s browser, signed in to the same Google account.

  3. 3

    Install it from Google Play

    On the same page, tap download it on Google Play, or use the button below. Daily Ring opens in the Play Store: tap Install.

    Daily Ring on Google Play

    “Item not found”? Joining can take a few minutes to reach the Play Store. Wait a little and try again, and check the account from step 1.

  4. 4

    Open Daily Ring and check something off

    There is no sign-up. Daily Ring opens with three example habits you can rename, change or delete. Tap a ring to check a habit off for today.

    Next: save your progress, then get Plus Lifetime free.

  5. ★

    Keep it for 14 days

    This is the part that matters most. Google lets a new app into the Play Store only after enough testers have stayed in its closed test for 14 days in a row. Please keep Daily Ring installed and stay opted in for at least that long.

    Using it every day helps even more: a few check-offs a day is a real test, and it gets you the confetti.

If something goes wrong

The link says I can’t join, or the app isn’t available

The link is open only to email addresses on the testers list. Check the account in the browser that opened it, or open the link in a private window and sign in with the right one. If it is the right account, tell us through the contact form so we can check the list.

I joined, but the Play Store still doesn’t show Daily Ring

Give it up to an hour, then close and reopen the Play Store. Searching for it may not work during a closed test, so use the Google Play button in step 3 instead.

My email isn’t a Gmail address

It needs to be the address of a Google account. Any email can be one: create a Google account with it, or send us your Gmail address instead.

How do I leave the test?

Open the test link again and tap Leave the program, then uninstall the app. If you added an email, delete the account first under Habits → Account → Delete account to remove its data; here is how.

Something odd, or an idea? Tell us. Every message is read, and the test exists to find exactly that.
