Privacy Policy

Daily Ring is a habit tracker. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. It collects only what it needs to back up your habits.

The short version

  • No sign-up is needed. The app gives you an anonymous account automatically.
  • Your habits and check-offs are backed up to our server so they survive a lost phone. Adding an email is optional.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app: Habits → Account → Delete account.

Who we are

Daily Ring (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed and published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy go through our contact form.

What we collect

Data When Why
An anonymous account ID (a random identifier) The first time the app goes online To keep your backup separate from everyone else’s
Your habits: names, chosen icons and colours, daily targets and units, and their order When you add or change a habit To back them up and sync them between your devices
Your check-offs: how many times each habit was done on each day When you log a habit To back up your history and streaks
Your current challenge (its length and start date) When you start a challenge To back it up with the rest of your progress
Email address and password Only if you choose to add them in the Account screen So you can sign in on another device. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.

Habit names are whatever you type. Please do not put sensitive information in them; the app treats them as ordinary text.

What stays on your device

Reminder settings, reminder times and whether you have finished the introduction never leave your device. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not use push notifications. The Home Screen widget and the Apple Watch app get their data directly from the app on your phone, not from our server.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, health data, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. There are no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

If you contact us

The contact form on this website asks for your email address and your message, and optionally your name and your device and app version. When you press Send, those details are passed to our email provider and delivered to our inbox as an email, so that we can reply to you. The form does not save them to a database, and this website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

We keep the email for as long as we need it to deal with your message, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

How we use it

Only to provide the app’s backup and sync, and to answer you when you contact us. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not train any models on it. We may email you only about your account, for example to confirm an email address you added, or in reply to a message you sent.

Who processes it

Backups are stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in service in the United States. Supabase processes the data only on our behalf. Like most online services it keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) to run and secure the service. Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the database only lets each account read and write its own rows.

Messages from the contact form pass through a Supabase function and are sent by our email provider, Resend, in the United States. Resend processes them only to deliver the email and keeps limited delivery records for a short time.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all its habits, check-offs and challenge are deleted from our server immediately and permanently, and removed from your device.

If you remove the app without deleting your account first, your data stays on our server. An anonymous account holds no email or anything else that identifies you, so we cannot match it to you afterwards. We periodically delete anonymous accounts that have not been used for 30 days, along with all their data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: delete your account and data at any time in the app, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Access or correct: everything we store is visible and editable in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Export: take a copy of every check-off at any time with Habits → Export data. The CSV file is made on your device and is shared only where you choose; it is never sent to us.
  • Stay offline: the app works without a network connection; nothing is sent until you are online.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your local data protection authority. We process your data because it is needed to provide the service you asked for. To exercise any right, use our contact form; we reply within 30 days.

Children

Daily Ring is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
