Delete your account

You can delete your Daily Ring account and everything stored with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Daily Ring and go to the Habits tab.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

This works on iPhone, iPad and Android, whether or not you added an email. You need to be online. The app then starts fresh with a new, empty account.

If you no longer have the app

Send us a request through the contact form, with Delete my account as the topic. Enter the email address you added to your Daily Ring account. We will email that address to confirm the request came from you, then delete the account and its data and let you know. This takes at most 30 days, and usually much less.

Request account deletion

If you never added an email, your account is anonymous: it holds nothing that lets us tell it apart from anyone else’s, so we cannot find it from an email. Reinstalling the app gives you a new account rather than your old one. Unused anonymous accounts are deleted automatically after 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address and password, if you added them
  • All your habits, with their names, icons, colours and targets
  • Every check-off and your full history and streaks
  • Your challenge
  • The copy of all of the above stored on the device you delete from

What is kept

Nothing from your account. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operations; these are not linked to your habits and expire on their own. If you were also signed in on another device, such as a second phone, its local copy stays on that device until you sign out there or remove the app.

See the privacy policy for more on what Daily Ring stores.
