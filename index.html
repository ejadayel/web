Help

Common questions

Is Daily Ring available in French?

Yes. Daily Ring follows your device’s language: French if your phone is set to French, English otherwise, including the widget and the Apple Watch app. To choose a language for Daily Ring alone, go to Settings → Daily Ring → Language on iPhone, or to App languages on Android 13 and later.

Do I need an account?

No. The app backs up your habits to an anonymous account automatically. To use the same habits on another device, or to keep them after changing phones, add an email in Habits → Account.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

On your old phone, add an email in Habits → Account. On the new phone, open the same screen and sign in with that email and password.

How do I set reminders?

Go to Habits → Reminders, turn them on and pick the times. Reminders stay on your device, so allow notifications for Daily Ring in your phone’s settings.

How do I reorder or edit habits?

On the Habits tab, touch and hold a habit, then drag it. The new order appears on every screen. Tap Edit on a habit to rename it or change its daily target.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Habits → Export data. Daily Ring makes a CSV file with one row for each habit on each day you logged it, then opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files or Drive, email it, or open it in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. The file is made on your device and goes only where you send it.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. It comes with the iPhone app: once Daily Ring is on your iPhone, install it on your watch from the App Store on the watch, or let it install automatically if Automatic App Install is on in the Watch app. It needs watchOS 10 or later, and anything you log on your wrist reaches your iPhone the next time they are connected.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline, and changes are backed up when you are next online.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Habits → Account → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see Delete your account.
