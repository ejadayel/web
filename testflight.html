Test Daily Ring with TestFlight

Thank you for testing. You are already on the list, so all that is left is five steps on your iPhone. It takes about five minutes.

iPhone and iPad Android
  • About 5 minutesFrom the invitation to your first check-off
  • Your iPhone or iPadAnd the email address you gave us
  • FreeTestFlight, the beta and test purchases cost nothing
  1. 1

    Install TestFlight

    TestFlight is Apple’s free app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. Open the App Store on your iPhone, search for TestFlight, and tap Get.

    It is published by Apple. You only need to install it once, and it works on iPad too.

  2. 2

    Open the invitation on your iPhone

    You will get an email from TestFlight inviting you to test Daily Ring, sent to the address you gave us. Open it on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight.

    Opened it on a computer? The email also has a redeem code. Open TestFlight on your iPhone, tap Redeem at the top, and type the code in.

  3. 3

    Accept, then install

    TestFlight opens on Daily Ring. Tap Accept, then Install. When it finishes, the button reads Open, and Daily Ring is on your Home Screen with a small orange dot beside its name, which marks a beta.

    The What to Test notes on the same screen say what changed in this build.

  4. 4

    Open Daily Ring and check something off

    There is no sign-up. Daily Ring opens with three example habits you can rename, change or delete. Tap a ring to check a habit off for today, and keep going until every habit is done.

    Next: save your progress, then get Plus Lifetime free.

  5. 5

    Turn on automatic updates

    In TestFlight, tap Daily Ring and switch on Automatic Updates. New builds then install by themselves, and you always test the latest one.

    Each build stops opening 90 days after it was uploaded. With automatic updates on, you will rarely see that happen.

While you test

Send feedback with a screenshot

Take a screenshot, tap its thumbnail, then tap Done and Share Beta Feedback. Add a note and send it. Your screenshot and note come straight to us.

Or open TestFlight, tap Daily Ring and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Plus is free for testers

Purchases in TestFlight are test purchases, never charged. Test subscriptions renew on a fast clock and end by themselves, so pick Lifetime.

The Apple Watch app

It comes with the beta. If it does not appear on your watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Daily Ring and tap Install. The watch app is part of Plus.

Your data carries over

The beta is the real app. When Daily Ring reaches the App Store, installing it from there replaces the beta and keeps your habits and history.

If something goes wrong

I can’t find the invitation email

Search your mail for “TestFlight”, and check the junk or spam folder. It goes to the email address you gave us. Still nothing after a day? Send us a message through the contact form and we will send it again.

“View in TestFlight” opens a web page instead

TestFlight is not installed yet, or the email was opened on a computer. Install TestFlight (step 1), then tap the button again on your iPhone, or use the redeem code in TestFlight’s Redeem button.

The invitation was sent to an address that isn’t my Apple Account

That is fine. The invitation works with whichever Apple Account is signed in on your iPhone; it does not need to match the email it was sent to.

TestFlight says the beta is full or no longer available

The build you were invited to has expired or been replaced. Open TestFlight again in a few minutes; if it still says so, tell us through the contact form.

How do I leave the beta?

In TestFlight, tap Daily Ring, then Stop Testing. You can delete the app as usual. If you added an email, delete the account first under Habits → Account → Delete account to remove its data; here is how.

Something odd, or an idea? Tell us. Every message is read, and the beta exists to find exactly that.
